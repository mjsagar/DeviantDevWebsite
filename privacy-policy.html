LEGAL & DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2025

Overview

Deviant Dev and its mobile applications, including DIY AI and Clean It AI ("the Apps"), are committed to safeguarding your privacy. We build software with on-device intelligence and zero unnecessary data collection.

Information We Collect

The Apps do NOT collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal data, usage tracking, or location telemetry from our users.

Third-Party Processing Services

When you initiate generative AI requests or tutorial searches, the Apps interface with the following specialized APIs strictly to fulfill your immediate request:

  • Google Gemini AI: Processes prompt text to synthesize DIY repair steps and maintenance routines.
  • YouTube API: Searches and returns relevant tutorial video embeds.
  • Groq AI: Low-latency backup inference service for fast text responses.
  • Google AdMob: Privacy-preserving advertisement displays where applicable.

Each service operates under its respective privacy framework:

  • Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • YouTube Terms of Service ↗
  • Groq Privacy Policy ↗

Local Data Security

Any user preferences or API credentials are stored securely on your physical device using the native iOS Keychain and isolated app sandboxes. No personal credentials are transmitted to Deviant Dev servers.

Children's Privacy

Due to the physical nature of tools, electrical systems, and home repair safety precautions, DIY AI is intended for users 18 years of age and older.

Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this policy as new features or platforms are deployed. Updates will always be published directly to this URL.
