IPTV Player Plus

Everything in IPTV Player, plus films and series from Stremio-style addons, in one app. Trailers play behind the details, intros and credits can be skipped, series carry on by themselves, and a setup wizard on your laptop gets it all going. Use it with an IPTV provider, with addons, or both.

ALL IN ONE

Live TV and Addons, Together

A player only: it ships no channels, streams or addons. You bring your provider and the addons you choose.

Stremio-Style Addons

Install addons by link, from your phone, or with the wizard. Their catalogues become shelves on Home, Movies and Series, and they're searched with everything else.

Browse by Service & Studio

Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and more, with each service's own originals; or Marvel, DC, Pixar, Lucasfilm, A24 and other studios. Sort by popular, newest or top rated.

Setup Wizard on Your Laptop

Open the address the TV shows: it creates your AIOMetadata catalogues, links your AIOStreams, adds subtitles, and saves setup files you can load on another TV.

Trailers in the Background

A title's trailer fades in behind its page after a few seconds, and when a film ends, a recommendation fills the screen with its trailer.

Skip Intro & Next Episode

Skip intros, recaps and credits using community timings. At the credits, the episode shrinks to the corner and the next one starts with a countdown.

Subtitles That Turn On

Subtitles from addons such as OpenSubtitles, by language, switched on by themselves in the languages you choose.

Everything from IPTV Player

The live TV guide, catch-up, recording, reminders, profiles and parental lock, and your provider's films and series, as in IPTV Player.

Your Home, Your Way

Save titles to My list, resume from Home or the TV's own Continue watching row, and choose which Home rows show and in what order.

Addons Only? Fine

No IPTV provider needed: choose "Only addons?" when you first start, and Home is made of your addons' shelves.

Direct Links Only

Plays direct video links, such as those from a service you subscribe to. Torrent links are never played.

INSTALL

Install on Your TV

Not on the Play Store yet, so it's installed ("sideloaded") as an APK. It installs alongside IPTV Player, not over it.

1

Get a downloader

On your Android TV or Google TV, install the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store.

2

Allow it to install apps

Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources, then turn on Downloader. (The exact path varies a little by TV.)

3

Download and install

Open Downloader and enter https://deviantdev.co.uk/iptv-player-plus/iptv-player-plus.apk, then choose Install.

4

Set it up

Add your IPTV provider as in IPTV Player, or choose Only addons? Set them up instead. For the full setup, open the address shown in Settings → Addons on a laptop on the same Wi-Fi.

5

Updates come to you

The app tells you when there's a new version and installs it for you. The first time, your TV asks to allow installs from IPTV Player Plus.
