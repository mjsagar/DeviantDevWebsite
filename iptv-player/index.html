IPTV Player

A fast, good-looking IPTV player for the big screen. Add your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login and get a live TV guide, catch-up, recording, movies and series, all built for the remote.

BUILT FOR THE REMOTE

Everything Live TV Should Be

A player only: it ships no channels or streams. Bring the service you already pay for.

A Guide That Keeps Up

Live video behind a smooth TV guide, genre and favourite filters, reminders, and real XMLTV listings from your provider or any guide link.

Catch-up & Start Over

Play programmes you missed straight from the guide, or restart the one that's on now, on providers that support catch-up.

Record to Anywhere

Record now or schedule from the guide. Save to the device, a USB drive, any folder, or a NAS on your network.

Movies & Series

Browse, resume and binge with Continue watching and Up next. Add free TMDB / TVDB keys for rich artwork, title logos and plots.

Proper 4K Playback

Frame-rate matching for judder-free films and sport, HDR10 / HLG / Dolby Vision detection, and picture size remembered per channel.

Set Up From Your Phone

Scan a QR code and paste long playlist links or keys on your phone instead of typing them with the remote.

INSTALL

Install on Your TV

Not on the Play Store yet, so it's installed ("sideloaded") as an APK.

1

Get a downloader

On your Android TV or Google TV, install the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store.

2

Allow it to install apps

Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources, then turn on Downloader. (The exact path varies a little by TV.)

3

Download and install

Open Downloader and enter https://deviantdev.co.uk/iptv-player/iptv-player.apk, then choose Install.

4

Add your service

Open IPTV Player, scan the QR code with your phone, and paste your M3U link or Xtream Codes details.
