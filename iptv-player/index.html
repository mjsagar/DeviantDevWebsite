IPTV Player

A fast, good-looking IPTV player for the big screen. Add your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login, or switch on free channels by country, and get a live TV guide, catch-up, recording, movies and series, all built for the remote.

BUILT FOR THE REMOTE

Everything Live TV Should Be

A player only: it ships no channels or streams. Bring the service you already pay for.

A Guide That Keeps Up

Live video behind a smooth TV guide with pictures for what's on, group and favourite filters, reminders that work even when the app is closed, and a Home row of the popular shows coming up on your channels.

Catch-up & Start Over

Start over the programme that's on, or pick an earlier one from Live TV options, on providers that support catch-up.

Record to Anywhere

Record now, schedule from the guide, or record every episode. Save to the device, a USB drive, any folder, or a NAS on your network.

Movies & Series

Browse, resume and binge with Continue watching and Up next, also in your TV's own Continue watching row. Add free TMDB / TVDB keys for rich artwork, title logos and plots.

Proper 4K Playback

Frame-rate matching for judder-free films and sport, HDR10 / HLG / Dolby Vision detection, and picture size remembered per channel.

Set Up From Your Phone

Scan a QR code and paste long playlist links or keys on your phone. Later, the same page is a remote with typing, and backs up your settings.

Free Channels, If You Want Them

No provider? Switch on free-to-air channels by country, with free TV guides. Off until you choose; the lists come from the community iptv-org project.

Profiles & Parental Lock

Everyone gets their own favourites and recently watched. Lock channel groups behind a PIN; kids' profiles never see them.

A Tidy Line-up

Find duplicate channels, hide or rename the rest, and when a channel keeps dropping out, get offered another version of it.

INSTALL

Install on Your TV

Not on the Play Store yet, so it's installed ("sideloaded") as an APK.

1

Get a downloader

On your Android TV or Google TV, install the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store.

2

Allow it to install apps

Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources, then turn on Downloader. (The exact path varies a little by TV.)

3

Download and install

Open Downloader and enter https://deviantdev.co.uk/iptv-player/iptv-player.apk, then choose Install.

4

Add your service

Open IPTV Player, scan the QR code with your phone, and paste your M3U link or Xtream Codes details. No service? Choose No provider? Try free channels.

5

Updates come to you

From version 0.4.0, the app tells you when there's a new version and installs it for you. The first time, your TV asks to allow installs from IPTV Player.
