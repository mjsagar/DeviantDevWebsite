1. Introduction

At Deviant Dev ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe that simple, single-purpose apps should respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Clothing Line (the "App") handles information when you install and use it on your iOS device.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you have any questions, you can reach us directly at info@deviantdev.com.

2. Location Data

The core functionality of Clothing Line is to notify you before rain showers hit your clothesline. Accurate weather prediction requires knowing your approximate geographical coordinates.

How Location Is Used

  • Hyper-Local Forecasting: When you start a drying session or check weather conditions, the App queries your device's location to fetch accurate precipitation and cloud cover data from Apple WeatherKit.
  • Background Updates: When a drying session is actively in progress ("Hang Out"), iOS may briefly wake the App in the background to inspect incoming rain radars for your location.
  • No Tracking or History: We do not log, retain, or map your movement history. Location coordinates are transmitted in encrypted form directly to Apple's weather services and discarded immediately after the forecast is processed.
  • Manual Address Option: If you prefer not to grant GPS location access, the App provides an option to manually enter a static address (e.g., your home address). All weather lookups will then be based strictly on that saved coordinate.

Control: You can grant, change, or revoke Location permissions at any time via your device: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Clothing Line.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless experience, the App stores certain preferences and state information locally on your device (using iOS UserDefaults and a private App Group container):

  • Active laundry session state (e.g., whether washing is currently out, time hung out).
  • User-selected warning lead time (e.g., 5, 15, or 30 minutes advance warning).
  • Cached weather snapshots for rendering iOS Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
  • Ad-free status or active in-app purchase receipts verified through Apple StoreKit.

None of this information is sent to or stored on any external server managed by Deviant Dev.

4. Push & Local Notifications

When you enable alerts, the App schedules Local Notifications directly through iOS (via UserNotifications framework) to alert you before rain is predicted.

  • All notification triggers are evaluated and generated directly on your iPhone.
  • We do not operate an external notification server and do not collect your Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) device token.
  • You can toggle notification permissions on or off at any time in Settings > Notifications > Clothing Line.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App integrates with select, reputable third-party services to deliver weather data, handle monetization, and provide ads on the free tier:

A. Apple WeatherKit

Weather forecasts, hourly precipitation data, wind, and sky condition metrics are provided by Apple WeatherKit. When weather data is requested, your device communicates with Apple servers. This data exchange is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

B. Apple StoreKit (In-App Subscriptions)

The App offers an optional auto-renewing "Remove Ads" subscription. All payment transactions, receipt verification, and renewals are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Deviant Dev never receives, processes, or stores your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

C. Google AdMob (Advertising)

The free tier of the App displays banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers — IDFA, subject to your App Tracking Transparency permission).
  • IP address and general coarse location (country/city level) for ad serving and fraud prevention.
  • Ad interaction data and technical diagnostic logs.

User Consent (Google UMP): In regions requiring consent (such as the EEA and UK under GDPR), the App presents Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can review or modify your consent preferences at any time by tapping "Privacy options" in the App's Settings screen.

Ad-Free Experience: Subscribing to "Remove Ads" completely disables the Google AdMob SDK and silences all ad network communications.

For further details on how Google uses data, visit Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Clothing Line is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you may have specific statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Because we do not operate user accounts or maintain personal databases, we hold no personal data linked to your identity.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can delete all locally cached data at any time simply by uninstalling the App from your iOS device.
  • Right to Opt Out of Tracking: You can deny App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission when prompted by iOS, or disable tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Right to Revoke Location / Notifications: You can modify or withdraw permissions at any time through standard iOS Settings.
