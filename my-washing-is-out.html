THE PROBLEM & THE SOLUTION

Stop Playing Russian Roulette With The Weather

Save your clothes from surprise drizzles, reduce dryer bills, and dry outside with total confidence.

THE FRUSTRATION

The Saturated Clothesline Dilemma

"You wash a big load of bedding and hang it in the sun. You head upstairs to work or pop out for coffee. An hour later, an unexpected drizzle drenches everything—forcing you to re-wash, waste energy, and lose hours of your weekend."

Unpredictable showers ruin fresh laundry
THE SOLUTION

One Tap. 100% Peace of Mind.

One tap on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Set your preferred alert window—say, 15 minutes before precipitation arrives. Go about your day with total confidence. If rain clouds gather over your garden, your phone warns you with ample time to bring your clothes inside.

Hyper-local alerts before the first drop
BUILT FOR PEACE OF MIND

Engineered for British & Global Skies

Every feature is crafted to make outdoor laundry effortless, reliable, and sustainable.

One-Tap Session Tracking

Tap "Hang Out" the moment your clothes hit the line. Tap "Bring In" when they're dry. No complicated setup, no fiddly menus, no stress.

Custom Warning Buffer (5–60 Mins)

Pick the lead time that fits your life. Upstairs in a Zoom meeting? Set 20 minutes. Just a quick step onto the patio? 5 minutes is plenty.

Hyper-Local Precision

Powered by high-resolution meteorological models tailored down to your exact garden coordinates, tracking rain intensity, probability, and wind speed.

Glanceable iOS Widgets

Keep an eye on your drying session, sky status, and rain countdown right from your iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen without even opening the app.

Dynamic Living Skies

The app's illustrated sky seamlessly shifts in real time to match the sunlight, cloud coverage, twilight, and impending fronts over your home.

Save Energy & Money

Tumble dryers are among the most power-hungry appliances in the house. Confident outdoor drying dramatically cuts electricity bills and helps the planet.
